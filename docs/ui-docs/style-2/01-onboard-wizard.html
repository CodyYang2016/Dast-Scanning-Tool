What this means

Why we ask about the "signed-in" page. During a scan we keep checking it. If it disappears, the session was lost — we sign in again or stop, rather than reporting "nothing found".

Why SSO is different. Single sign-on and MFA can't be automated safely, so a person signs in once and we reuse that session until it expires.
